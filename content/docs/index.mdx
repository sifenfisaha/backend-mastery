Welcome. You already ship features as a mid-level full-stack developer. This program closes the gap
between *"I can build it"* and *"I can design, scale, secure and operate it"* — the gap that
separates mid-level from senior.

It covers **17 systems**, each with a plain-English mental model, the core concepts, **verified video
courses you can play right here**, the best free written resources, a **build-this lab**, and an
**interactive mastery checklist** that saves your progress in your browser.

<MentorNote>
Read this like a coach is sitting next to you. I'll tell you *what matters*, *what to skip*, and
*what to build*. Knowledge you don't apply evaporates in a week — so every topic ends with something
you make with your own hands. Do the labs. That's where mastery actually happens.
</MentorNote>

## The fastest way to use this

<Steps>
<Step>
**Read the [90-day roadmap](/docs/roadmap) first.** It sequences every topic week-by-week so you're
never learning something before its prerequisites.
</Step>
<Step>
**Pick the current week's topic.** Watch the short "in 100 seconds" video for the mental model, then
one deep-dive course.
</Step>
<Step>
**Do the lab.** Every page has a "Build this" section. Ship it, then tick the mastery checklist.
</Step>
<Step>
**Move on only when you can explain it out loud** — to a rubber duck, a friend, or an imaginary
interviewer.
</Step>
</Steps>

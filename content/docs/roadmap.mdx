This is the plan. Thirteen weeks, three phases, one build target per week. It's sequenced so you
never hit a topic before its prerequisites. Budget **~10–12 focused hours a week** — roughly 90
minutes a day. If you have less time, don't skip topics; stretch the plan to 5 months instead.

<MentorNote title="How to actually finish this">
The people who complete a plan like this aren't the smartest — they're the ones who **build every
week** and **never let two weeks pass without shipping something**. Watching videos feels like
progress but isn't. The lab is the progress. Treat the videos as *just enough* to unblock the build.
</MentorNote>

## The rhythm of a week

<Steps>
<Step>**Mon–Tue — Absorb.** Watch the mental-model video + one deep dive on the topic page.</Step>
<Step>**Wed–Thu — Build.** Do that topic's "Build this" lab. Break it on purpose. Fix it.</Step>
<Step>**Fri — Consolidate.** Tick the mastery checklist. Write 5 sentences explaining the topic from memory.</Step>
<Step>**Weekend — Optional.** Rest, or push the build further. Recovery is part of the plan.</Step>
</Steps>

## Phase 1 · Month 1 — Foundations & Data

> Goal: be completely comfortable in a Linux shell, containerize anything, and model + query data
> like you mean it. Everything later stands on this.

<Roadmap>
<Phase title="Month 1 — Foundations & Data" subtitle="Weeks 1–4 · the ground everything stands on">
<Week n={1} title="Linux & Networking">
Live in the terminal. Learn processes, permissions, pipes, and the network path a request actually
travels: DNS → TCP → TLS → HTTP.
- Topic: [Linux & Networking](/docs/foundations/linux-networking)
- **Build:** a bash script that health-checks a URL (DNS resolve, TCP connect, TLS expiry, HTTP status) and logs results.
</Week>
<Week n={2} title="Docker">
Containerize a real app. Understand images vs containers, layers, volumes, networks, and Compose.
- Topic: [Docker](/docs/foundations/docker)
- **Build:** dockerize one of your existing apps + its database with a single `docker compose up`.
</Week>
<Week n={3} title="PostgreSQL">
Schema design, the right indexes, transactions, and reading a query plan with `EXPLAIN ANALYZE`.
- Topic: [PostgreSQL](/docs/data/postgresql)
- **Build:** design a normalized schema for a small product (e.g. a blog or store), seed it, and make three slow queries fast with indexes.
</Week>
<Week n={4} title="Redis + Caching">
In-memory data structures, then caching patterns: cache-aside, TTLs, and invalidation.
- Topics: [Redis](/docs/data/redis) · [Caching](/docs/data/caching)
- **Build (milestone):** add a Redis cache-aside layer in front of a slow Postgres query and measure the latency drop. Now you have a Dockerized API + Postgres + Redis — your Phase-1 stack.
</Week>
</Phase>

<Phase title="Month 2 — Architecture & Messaging" subtitle="Weeks 5–9 · think in systems, not files">
<Week n={5} title="System Design">
Learn the framework: requirements → estimates → high-level design → deep dives → bottlenecks.
- Topic: [System Design](/docs/architecture/system-design)
- **Build:** write a 2-page design doc for a URL shortener (API, schema, scale estimates, caching).
</Week>
<Week n={6} title="Distributed Systems">
The hard truths: partial failure, CAP, replication, consistency, consensus (Raft), consistent hashing.
- Topic: [Distributed Systems](/docs/architecture/distributed-systems)
- **Build:** implement consistent hashing in code and simulate adding/removing nodes; observe key movement.
</Week>
<Week n={7} title="Microservices">
Service boundaries, sync vs async communication, data ownership — and when a monolith is the right call.
- Topic: [Microservices](/docs/architecture/microservices)
- **Build:** split one feature out of your Phase-1 app into its own service with its own database.
</Week>
<Week n={8} title="Load Balancing + Scalability & Reliability">
L4 vs L7 balancing, health checks, then redundancy, circuit breakers, retries, and SLOs.
- Topics: [Load Balancing](/docs/architecture/load-balancing) · [Scalability & Reliability](/docs/architecture/scalability-reliability)
- **Build:** run 3 instances of a service behind NGINX; add a circuit breaker + retry/backoff around a flaky dependency.
</Week>
<Week n={9} title="Queues + Kafka/RabbitMQ">
Async processing, decoupling, dead-letter queues, idempotency — with real brokers.
- Topics: [Queues & Event-Driven](/docs/messaging/queues-eda) · [Kafka & RabbitMQ](/docs/messaging/kafka-rabbitmq)
- **Build (milestone):** an event-driven order pipeline — `OrderPlaced` fans out to email + inventory consumers, with a DLQ and idempotent handlers.
</Week>
</Phase>

<Phase title="Month 3 — Platform, Ops & Quality" subtitle="Weeks 10–13 · run it in production like a senior">
<Week n={10} title="Kubernetes">
Pods, deployments, services, ingress, config/secrets, and autoscaling on a local cluster.
- Topic: [Kubernetes](/docs/platform/kubernetes)
- **Build:** deploy your event-driven app to a local k8s cluster (kind/minikube) with an ingress and an HPA.
</Week>
<Week n={11} title="AWS & Azure">
Core cloud primitives: compute, storage, networking, IAM, and the managed versions of everything you built by hand.
- Topic: [AWS & Azure](/docs/platform/cloud)
- **Build:** deploy a small service to a free-tier account (S3 static site + a Lambda/Function, or a managed container).
</Week>
<Week n={12} title="CI/CD + Observability">
Automated pipelines with test gates, then logs/metrics/traces and a real dashboard + alert.
- Topics: [CI/CD](/docs/platform/cicd) · [Observability](/docs/platform/observability)
- **Build:** a GitHub Actions pipeline that tests + builds + deploys, plus a Prometheus/Grafana dashboard for your app.
</Week>
<Week n={13} title="Security + Testing">
OWASP Top 10, auth done right, secrets handling — and a test suite you actually trust.
- Topics: [Security](/docs/quality/security) · [Testing](/docs/quality/testing)
- **Build (capstone):** harden your app against the OWASP Top 10 and get it to a meaningful test pyramid. Then write it all up.
</Week>
</Phase>
</Roadmap>

## After week 13

You now have a portfolio system that is containerized, event-driven, deployed to Kubernetes and the
cloud, monitored, tested and secured. Turn it into the [**capstone write-up**](/docs/capstones) and
you can walk into a senior interview and defend every layer.

<Callout type="warn">
Falling behind is normal. If a week runs long, **extend the timeline — don't skip the build.** A
half-understood topic you built beats five topics you only watched.
</Callout>
